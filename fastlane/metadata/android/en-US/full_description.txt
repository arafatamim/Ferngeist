Run Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Pi or another coding agent on your computer and drive it from your Android phone or tablet. Start a task from the couch, and Ferngeist notifies you when the agent finishes.

Ferngeist works with any agent that speaks the Agent Client Protocol (ACP).

What you need: a computer running your agent, plus the free Ferngeist Gateway (Windows, macOS or Linux). The gateway finds the agents you have installed and gives your phone one secure address to reach them. Ferngeist has no AI model of its own.

Features:
• Read messages, reasoning, tool calls and code diffs in a layout made for a small screen
• Browse the project's git changes and history
• Sessions reconnect where they left off after a dropped connection
• Notifications that open the chat when an agent finishes or needs an answer
• One pane on a phone, two on a small tablet, three on a large one
• Free and open source (MIT), no account, and no servers of our own

Source and setup guide: https://github.com/arafatamim/ferngeist
